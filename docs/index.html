Dutch municipal parking for Home Assistant

One municipality.
Live zones, tariffs & decisions.

NPR Parkeren reads the Nationaal Parkeer Register and the municipal gazette. See paid, permit and blue zones, the rate and maximum stay in force now, whether your coordinates are inside a zone, and recent traffic decisions.

Install with HACS Latest release

What you get

One entry per municipality

The area manager is looked up during setup. The same municipality cannot be added twice.

Tariff in force now

Paid, permit and blue zones from the RDW national parking register, with the rate and maximum stay for the current weekday and time.

Can I park here?

A sensor answers whether your configured coordinates sit inside a regulated street within the chosen radius.

Traffic decisions

Municipal gazette items in the verkeersbesluit rubriek, with publication text from the official XML and neighbour hits filtered out.

Capped map pins

Device-tracker pins coloured by usage. Full polygons stay in the coordinator cache; entities publish a centroid and at most twelve ring points.

Street watch

List streets you care about. A new decision that names one of them fires an update you can automate on.

Zero-bloat by design

NPR tables are filtered to the area manager of the entry and cached for 12 hours. A failed poll keeps the last good payload. Fat overview attributes should stay out of the recorder.

  • Radius and map-marker cap in options
  • Pins outside the cap are removed immediately
  • Coordinator-first, single poll cycle
recorder:
  exclude:

Install

  1. 1HACS → Integrations → ⋮ → Custom repositories
  2. 2URL: https://github.com/DonTranQuiL/NPR-Parkeren-for-Home-Assistant · Category: Integration
  3. 3Download NPR Parkeren, then restart Home Assistant
  4. 4Settings → Devices & services → Add Integration → NPR Parkeren
  5. 5Enter municipality, latitude, longitude and radius. The area manager is resolved during setup.

Lovelace card

Copy npr_parkeren-card.js into /config/www/, add a resource /local/npr_parkeren-card.js (JavaScript Module), then:

type: custom:npr_parkeren-card
entity: sensor.npr_parkeren_maastricht_overview
title: Parking near home
